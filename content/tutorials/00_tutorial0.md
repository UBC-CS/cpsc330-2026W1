# Tutorial 0: Environment Setup and Intro to HW1

By the end of this tutorial, you will be fully set up and will have completed at least one question from Homework 1 (HW1), either locally or in PrairieLearn.

## Before the Tutorial
To make the most of our time, please do the following before arriving:

- Review the [setup.md](../../docs/setup.md) file for software requirements and installation steps.
  - Try to get your local environment ready. If you hit a roadblock, do not worry. Bring your laptop and the error messages so the TAs can help.
- Verify you can log into our course PrairieLearn.

## During the Tutorial

1. Environment Setup and Troubleshooting
   - If you have not finished setting up your environment, use this time to do so.
   - TAs will walk around to help resolve any installation issues you encountered with [setup](../../docs/setup.md).

2. Hands-On: First HW1 Question
   - Once your setup is complete, you will work independently on HW1. Your objective is to finish and submit at least one question.
   - TAs will be available to answer questions and help you get started with the assignment and workflow.
